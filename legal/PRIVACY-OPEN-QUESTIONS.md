## Review guidance

The policy describes implemented processing and labels operational gaps. It does not invent a legal basis, data-subject-right deadline, regulator, transfer mechanism, certification, retention period or absolute non-sale promise. Retention placeholders must not be used to conceal the known indefinite deletion-record behavior.

For a jurisdictional assessment, the ICO's [privacy information guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/the-right-to-be-informed/what-privacy-information-should-we-provide/) identifies matters such as controller details, purposes, legal bases, recipients, transfers, retention and rights. It is relevant as a review checklist only; UK law applicability is unconfirmed.
